About G-WAC

German-West African Centre for Global Health and Pandemic Prevention

Our Mission

The Centre aims to address the existential threat of pandemics to the health and welfare of people through trans- and inter-disciplinary research projects targeting both the main drivers of pandemics (e.g. health impact of wild habitat encroachment, extensive agriculture and climate change, transfer of pathogens from wild animals to humans) and the key pillars of resilient health systems in the WHO framework.

Course Overview

Comprehensive curriculum covering essential topics in mathematical modeling and public health

What You'll Learn

Our intensive 9-day program covers essential and advanced disease modeling skills for pandemic preparedness and response:

Infectious Disease Modeling

Understand infectious disease transmission and control, design SIR/SEIR/SEIRV models, and calculate basic reproduction numbers.

R Programming & Simulation

Master R programming, use deSolve package, write ODE systems, implement model simulation with odin & monty packages.

Data Curation & Model Fitting

Learn data sources, access challenges, model calibration techniques, and validation methods for African contexts.

Group Projects & Communication

Work on real projects (COVID-19, Malaria, Schistosomiasis) and develop science communication skills.

Professional Network

Join the G-WAC MPPR Network of Modelers for Pandemic Preparedness and Response in Africa.
