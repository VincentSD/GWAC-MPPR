Course Overview

Comprehensive curriculum covering essential topics in mathematical modeling and public health

What You'll Learn

Our intensive 2-week program covers five core areas that will transform your understanding of mathematical modeling in public health:

Mathematical Foundations

Master differential equations, parameter estimation, and model validation techniques essential for epidemiological modeling.

Epidemiological Modeling

Explore advanced techniques including SIR models, stochastic processes, and network-based approaches to disease dynamics.

Computational Methods

Develop programming skills in R, data visualization, statistical analysis, and machine learning applications.

Data Analysis & Visualization

Learn data cleaning, exploratory analysis, statistical inference, and effective communication of results.

Collaborative Research

Engage in group projects, peer learning, scientific communication, and policy-relevant research applications.
